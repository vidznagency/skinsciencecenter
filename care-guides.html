Before treatment

Before your laser session

  • Avoid sun exposure and tanning for 2 weeks before your session.
  • Shave the area 12–24 hours before laser hair reduction. Do not wax, thread or pluck for 4 weeks.
  • Stop retinoids and exfoliating acids on the area 3–5 days before, unless told otherwise.
  • Tell the doctor about any new medicines, cold sores or recent illness.
  • Arrive with clean skin — no makeup, creams or deodorant on the area.
After treatment

After your chemical peel

  • Expect mild redness, tightness or light flaking for a few days. Do not pick or peel the skin.
  • Use a gentle cleanser and the moisturiser recommended by the doctor.
  • Apply broad-spectrum sunscreen every morning and reapply every 2–3 hours outdoors.
  • Avoid scrubs, retinoids, acids, waxing and facials for 5–7 days.
  • Avoid heavy sweating, steam and swimming for 48 hours.
After treatment

After PRP / GFC therapy

  • Do not wash your hair for the rest of the day, or as advised.
  • Mild tenderness or a dull ache is normal for a day.
  • Avoid hair colouring, chemical treatments and heavy exercise for 48–72 hours.
  • Continue your prescribed hair medicines unless told otherwise.
After treatment

After fillers & anti-wrinkle injections

  • Do not massage or press the treated areas for 24 hours (unless instructed).
  • Stay upright for 4 hours after anti-wrinkle injections. Avoid heavy exercise for 24 hours.
  • Mild swelling or small bruises can occur and settle within days.
  • Contact the clinic immediately if you notice increasing pain, skin colour change (white or dusky patches) or vision changes after fillers.
  • Attend your review appointment at 2 weeks.
