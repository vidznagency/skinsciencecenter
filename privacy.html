What we collect

When you book an appointment or subscribe, we collect the details you choose to share: your name, mobile number, email, age, your skin concern, preferred appointment time and any photos you upload.

How we use it

Only to arrange and provide your care: confirming appointments, preparing for your consultation and, if you subscribed, sending our newsletter. We never sell your data.

Photos

Photos you upload are seen only by the doctor and clinical team. Before-and-after images are published only with a patient's separate written consent.

Third-party services

This site uses Google Maps (map embed), Google Fonts and, if you choose, WhatsApp to send your booking. These services have their own privacy policies.
